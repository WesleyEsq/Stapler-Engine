Stapler Engine — Concept Art & Visual Style Reference
=====================================================

Visual Style:
All characters and environment cutouts use a 2.5D paper-diorama aesthetic with
a white sticker/cutout border and cast shadows against 3D lit planes.
- Committed production character style: Lineless, flat-shaded geometric SVG
  vectors (see `../../Unua.svg`), optimized for fast solo production, modular
  expressions/animation, and crisp high-DPI GPU rasterization.
- Early concept sheets in this folder explore Ada Nudos's poses, walk cadence,
  and layered 2.5D diorama lighting/staging.

Files in this folder:
- Scenario Style.jpeg
    Visual, lighting, and paper-cutout material style reference (demonstrating
    layered 2.5D depth, white cutout edges, and ground drop shadows — not a
    literal location from the test game).
- walking.jpeg
    Ada Nudos — 4-frame front/3/4 walk cycle (Contact Left, Passing, Contact
    Right, Passing).
- walking_back.jpeg
    Ada Nudos — 4-frame back walk cycle matching the front cycle cadence.
- emotion_1.jpeg
    Ada Nudos — Character concept sheet & base poses (1. Neutral Idle through
    5. Celebration).
- emotion_2.jpeg
    Ada Nudos — Additional dialogue & exploration poses (6. Concentration
    through 10. Inspecting Clues).
